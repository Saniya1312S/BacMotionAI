Physics Simulation Engine
Realistic E. coli dynamics
Each bacterium moves via run-and-tumble motion with hydrodynamic wall interactions, chemical modulation, and biological noise.
Speed v₀20 µm/s
Rot. Diff Dᵣ0.2 rad²/s
Torque κ6.0
Cell Radius1.0 µm
Time Step0.01 s
Duration6.0 s
θ(t+dt) = θ + ω·dt + drift·dt + √(2·Dᵣ·dt)·η
ω = -κ·(a/h)²·sin(2θ)  [hydro]
v = v₀·v_scale·(1 + ε·a/h)  [near wall]

14 measured statistics per bacterium

5 emergent motility regimes
Regimes are classified from measured behavior, not input parameters — preventing data leakage.

Slow, erratic, short runs (antibiotic-like)
Wall-Guided
High wall accumulation + alignment
Chemotactic
Long runs, directed motion
Confined
Frequent wall collisions
Free-Swimming
Open exploration, moderate speed
